### 1.2 Detailed Revenue Streams

#### A. Featured Listings (Transaction-Based)

| Product | Description | Price (EGP) | Price (USD est.) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Standard Listing** | Free listing with video, appears in organic feed | Free | Free |
| **Boost** | 3x visibility for 7 days, priority in search results | 150 - 300 | $3 - $6 |
| **Spotlight** | Top of feed for 24 hours in selected area, highlighted border | 500 - 1,000 | $10 - $20 |
| **Featured Listing** | 14-day priority placement + "Featured" badge + push to matching users | 750 - 1,500 | $15 - $30 |
| **Premium Listing** | 30-day top placement + social media promotion + dedicated landing page | 2,000 - 4,000 | $40 - $80 |

**Pricing Strategy:**
- Prices vary by city/district (New Cairo premium > Faisal)
- Introductory 50% discount for first 3 months
- Bundle discounts: 5 boosts for price of 4, 10 for price of 7
- Dynamic pricing based on demand (high-demand areas cost more)

#### B. Social Content Revenue (NEW in v3.0)

| Product | Description | Price (EGP) | Price (USD est.) |
|---------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Promoted Post** | Boost a social post's visibility to wider audience (non-followers) | 50 - 200 | $1 - $4 |
| **Group Sponsorship** | Brand sponsors a neighborhood group (banner, pinned post) | 2,000 - 5,000/month | $40 - $100 |
| **Expert Consultation** | Verified experts offer paid 1-on-1 advice sessions via chat | Platform takes 15% | Variable |
| **Content Tipping** | Users can tip helpful contributors (platform takes 10%) | Min. 10 EGP | ~$0.20 min |
| **Sponsored Group Post** | Developer/brand publishes sponsored content in relevant groups | 500 - 2,000 per post | $10 - $40 |
| **Priority Approval** | Members pay for faster review of their posts (< 15 min guarantee) | 25 EGP per post | ~$0.50 |

**Social Revenue Strategy:**
- Promoted posts are the highest-potential revenue stream for social (low cost, high volume)
- Group sponsorships target real estate developers wanting exposure in specific neighborhoods
- Tipping creates a creator economy — top contributors earn, platform takes a cut
- Priority approval monetizes the permission system without making it pay-to-play (free approval still available)

#### C. Contributor Subscriptions (NEW in v3.0)

| Tier | Features | Price/Month (EGP) | Price/Month (USD) |
|------|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Free Contributor** | Standard posting (with approval for Tier 3), basic profile | Free | Free |
| **Contributor Pro** | Priority in feed algorithm, analytics on post performance, exclusive "Pro" badge, early access to features, AI writing assistant (unlimited) | 100 | ~$2 |
| **Contributor Expert** | All Pro features + featured in "Expert" directory, offer paid consultations, pinned expert AMA sessions in groups, portfolio page | 300 | ~$6 |

#### D. Agent Subscriptions (Updated)

| Tier | Features | Price/Month (EGP) | Price/Month (USD) |
|------|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Free Agent** | 5 active listings, basic profile, WhatsApp link | Free | Free |
| **Agent Pro** | 25 active listings, verified badge priority, lead analytics, 2 free boosts/month, response time tracking, priority support | 500 | ~$10 |
| **Agent Premium** | 100 active listings, featured agent in search, advanced analytics dashboard, 5 free boosts/month, competitor insights, auto-response templates, branded profile page | 1,200 | ~$24 |
| **Agency Enterprise** | Unlimited listings, team management (up to 20 agents), aggregated analytics, bulk upload, API access, dedicated account manager, custom branding | 3,000 | ~$60 |

**Subscription Strategy:**
- Free tier is generous enough to attract agents (5 listings is enough for individual sellers)
- Pro tier hits the sweet spot for active agents (most agents have 10-25 properties)
- Annual billing: 20% discount (lock-in + cash flow)
- 14-day free trial of Pro for new agents
- Referral program: refer 3 agents → 1 month free Pro

#### E. Advertising Revenue (Updated for Social)

| Ad Format | Placement | Pricing Model | Est. Rate |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Sponsored Post** | Every 8th-10th post in feed, labeled "Sponsored" | CPM (cost per 1000 impressions) | 30-80 EGP CPM |
| **Sponsored Listing** | Top of search results, labeled "Ad" | CPC (cost per click) | 3-8 EGP CPC |
| **In-Feed Video Ad** | Native video ad in feed between posts | CPV (cost per view) | 5-15 EGP CPV |
| **Developer Partnership** | Branded section for real estate developers | Monthly flat fee | 10,000-50,000 EGP/month |
| **Group Sponsored Content** | Branded content pinned in relevant groups | Flat fee per group/week | 1,000-3,000 EGP/week |
| **Suggested Group Card** | "Suggested Group" card in feed (1 per 15 posts) | CPC | 5-12 EGP CPC |
| **Splash Ad** | Full-screen interstitial (max 1/session, skippable) | CPM | 80-150 EGP CPM |

**Ad Policy (v3.0):**
- Maximum 1 sponsored post per 8 organic posts in feed
- Maximum 1 suggested group per 15 posts
- All ads clearly labeled "Sponsored" or "Ad"
- No auto-play audio on ads
- Users can "Not interested" to reduce ad frequency
- No ads in chat or DMs
- No ads in post detail comments section
- Developer ads only for verified developers
- Sponsored group content must be relevant to group topic

## 2. Payment Infrastructure

### 2.1 Egypt Payment Landscape

| Payment Method | Market Share | Implementation | Priority |
|----------------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| **Fawry** | ~35% of digital payments | Fawry API / Paymob integration | P0 |
| **Vodafone Cash** | ~25% of mobile wallets | Paymob integration | P0 |
| **Credit/Debit Card** | ~20% (growing) | Paymob / Accept by Paymob | P0 |
| **Etisalat Cash** | ~10% | Paymob integration | P1 |
| **Orange Cash** | ~5% | Paymob integration | P1 |
| **InstaPay** | Growing rapidly | Direct bank API | P1 |
| **Aman** | ~5% | Paymob integration | P2 |

### 2.2 Payment Architecture

```
Payment Flow:
User selects plan/boost → Choose payment method
    │
    ├── Card Payment → Paymob iframe → 3D Secure → Webhook → Activate
    ├── Fawry → Generate reference code → User pays at kiosk → Webhook → Activate
    ├── Vodafone Cash → Redirect to wallet → OTP confirm → Webhook → Activate
    └── InstaPay → Generate QR/link → Bank transfer → Webhook → Activate

Backend:
- Paymob as primary payment orchestrator (supports all methods)
- Webhook-based confirmation (never trust client-side)
- Idempotency keys for all payment operations
- Receipt generation and email confirmation
- Automatic retry for failed webhooks
- Refund API for disputed charges
```

## 5. Branding Guidelines

### 5.1 Brand Identity

**Name:** Beitco (بيتكو)
- "Beit" (بيت) = Home in Arabic
- "co" = Company/Community
- Memorable, short, domain-available (.com, .app)
- **v3.0 emphasis:** "co" now leans into "Community" — Beitco is your real estate community

**Tagline Options (v3.0 — Social Network):**
1. "مجتمعك العقاري" (Your real estate community) — **Primary tagline**
2. "ناقش، اكتشف، واسكن" (Discuss, discover, and settle)
3. "بيتك مع المجتمع" (Your home, with the community)
4. "خبرات عقارية حقيقية" (Real real estate expertise)

**Brand Voice:**
- Friendly, conversational Arabic (عامية مصرية for marketing, فصحى for formal)
- Trustworthy but not corporate
- Community-oriented — "we" and "together" language
- Tech-savvy but accessible
- Egyptian humor where appropriate

**Logo Direction:**
- Modern, clean mark combining house + community/people/conversation
- Works at small sizes (app icon, favicon)
- Bilingual version (Arabic + English)
- Color: Primary Blue (#1A56DB) on white, white on dark backgrounds
